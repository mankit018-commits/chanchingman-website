Mr. Tindy Kwong 鄺天狄先生

Legal Executive法律行政人員

Biography簡歷

Mr. Kwong has worked in the legal industry for over 30 years. Mr. Kwong joined our firm in 2021 as a Legal Executive. He obtained his Diploma in Legal Studies from the University of Hong Kong, School of Professional and Continuing Education (HKU SPACE) in 2009, and was admitted as a Legal Executive in the same year.

He is primarily responsible for legal research, file and case management, client representation, as well as drafting and reviewing a broad range of legal documents and correspondence.

Mr. Kwong possesses extensive experience handling civil and criminal litigation across all levels of courts in Hong Kong, including the Magistrates' Court, District Court, High Court, and various appellate courts.

In terms of civil and commercial litigation, his expertise spans contractual disputes, shareholders' disputes, breach of directors' duties, debt recovery, employment disputes, personal injuries, employees' compensation, bankruptcy and insolvency matters, as well as land and property issues (including landlord and tenant disputes, adverse possession, and building management disputes). Further, he excels in family law matters, such as divorce, child care, custody, ancillary relief, and financial settlements.

In criminal law, he has extensive defence experience handling a broad range of offences, including theft, fraud and deception, assault and wounding, sexual offences, access to computer with dishonest intent, dangerous driving, trafficking in dangerous drugs, money laundering, false trademark, and import and export offences.

To date, Mr. Kwong has actively handled and represented clients in over 3,500 litigation matters, including more than 2,000 civil, commercial, and family cases, as well as over 1,500 criminal cases across various court levels.

Mr. Kwong is dedicated, meticulous, and effective legal support tailored to each client's specific needs.

Mr. Kwong is fluent in Cantonese, English, and Mandarin.

鄺天狄先生投身法律行業逾30年。他於2021年加入本行擔任法律行政人員。他於2009年獲香港大學專業進修學院（HKU SPACE）頒發法律研究文憑，並於同年獲認可為法律行政人員。

他主要負責法律研究、案件及檔案管理、代表客戶處理事務，以及起草和審閱各類法律文件與往來函件。

鄺天狄先生在處理香港各級法院的民事及刑事訴訟方面擁有豐富經驗，包括裁判法院、區域法院、高等法院以及各級上訴法院。在民商事訴訟範疇，他的專業領域涵蓋合約糾紛、股東爭議、違反董事信託責任、追討債務、勞資糾紛、人身傷亡、僱員補償（工傷）、破產及清盤事宜，以及土地與物業糾紛（包括業主與租客爭議、逆權侵佔和物業管理糾紛）。此外，他精通婚姻家事法各類事宜，如離婚、子女撫養、管養權、附屬濟助及財產分配等。

在刑事方面，他具備豐富的辯護經驗，處理過各類罪行，包括但不限於盜竊、欺詐及騙案、普通襲擊及傷人、性罪行、不誠實取用電腦、危險駕駛、販運危險藥物、洗黑錢、虛假商標以及進出口相關罪行。

迄今為止，鄺天狄先生已積極處理並代表客戶處理超過3,500宗訴訟案件，當中包括逾2,000宗民事、商事及家事案件，以及在各級法院審理的1,500多宗刑事案件。

鄺天狄先生工作專注熱誠，致力因應每位客戶的具體需求，提供具策略性且高效的法律支援。

鄺天狄先生能操流利的英語、廣東話及普通話。
