Alex 李旻軒律師

Associate Solicitor助理律師

Biography簡歷

Alex joined our firm as a Trainee Solicitor in July 2022 and was admitted as a Solicitor of the High Court of Hong Kong in October 2024. Currently an Associate with the firm, he brings four years of diverse experience in the legal industry.

Alex holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) from the University of Kent and a Master of Laws (LLM) in Intellectual Property Law from Queen Mary University of London. He subsequently completed his Postgraduate Certificate of Laws (PCLL) at the City University of Hong Kong.

Prior to joining our firm, Alex gained his first exposure to the legal industry during his two-year tenure at a global provider of legal information solutions. In this capacity, he delivered over 200 bilingual legal research training workshops to a broad spectrum of legal professionals, including international and local law firms, independent barristers, corporate in-house legal departments, government counsels, and academic institutions. He also actively contributed to the internal research and development of market-leading online legal research platforms widely utilized by Hong Kong legal practitioners in their daily practice.

Alex possesses a robust litigation and dispute resolution practice, with specialized expertise across family law, property disputes, commercial asset recovery, and international arbitration:

Matrimonial & Family Law: Experienced in handling comprehensive family law proceedings, including consent applications, ancillary relief, and children matters. Property Disputes: Advised on property disputes, with specific experience handling water leakage claims and building management disputes. Debt Recovery & Civil Fraud: Proven track record in high-value debt collection, asset tracing, and multi-jurisdictional fraud recovery. Arbitration & Cross-Border Applications: Experienced in institutional arbitration and court applications brought in support of international arbitration proceedings. Personal Injury & Medical Negligence: Advised private clients on tortious liability, personal injury actions, and medical negligence claims.

Alex advises a diverse portfolio of institutional and private clients on complex cross-border litigation matters across Hong Kong, Mainland China, and international markets. His practice focuses on representing Hong Kong, Mainland Chinese, and foreign high-net-worth individuals and corporations, which include but are not limited to Australian superannuation funds, a BVI company owned by members of a high-net-worth family in Hong Kong, a Singapore subsidiary of a global elastomers distributor headquartered in Luxembourg, a Russian state-owned special purpose vehicle, an Arab wholesale trading conglomerate specializing in the international distribution of petrochemical materials and crude oil, and a major national commercial bank in Mainland China.

Alex is proficient in English, Cantonese, and Mandarin.

李旻軒律師於 2022 年 7 月加入本行擔任實習律師，並於 2024 年 10 月獲認許為香港高等法院執業律師。他現為本所助理律師，在法律行業擁有四年的多元化經驗。

李旻軒律師持有英國肯特大學（University of Kent）法律學士學位（LLB）及英國倫敦大學瑪麗王后學院（Queen Mary University of London）知識產權法法律碩士學位（LLM），隨後於香港城市大學取得法學專業證書（PCLL）。

在加入本行之前，李旻軒律師在一家領先的全球法律資訊方案供應商任職兩年，初次涉足法律行業。在此期間，他為廣泛的法律專業人士舉辦了 200 多場雙語法律檢索培訓工作坊，對象包括國際及本地律師事務所、大律師、企業內部法律顧問、政府律師以及學術機構等。此外，他亦積極參與市場領先的線上法律檢索平台的內部研究與開發，該等平台現已廣為香港法律從業員於日常實務中應用。

擁有穩健的訴訟與爭議解決執業經驗，並在家事法、物業爭議、商業資產追收及國際仲裁領域具備專業知識：

婚姻及家事法：在處理全面的家事法法律程序方面經驗豐富，包括同意申請、附屬濟助及子女相關事宜。 物業爭議：曾就物業爭議提供法律顧問服務，並在處理滲水索償及大廈管理爭議方面具備專門經驗。 債務追收及民事欺詐：在高額債務催收、資產追查及跨國欺詐追收方面擁有廣受認可的往績紀錄。 仲裁及跨境申請：在機構仲裁及為支持國際仲裁程序而向法院提出的申請方面具備經驗。 人身傷害及醫療疏忽：曾就侵權責任、人身傷害訴訟及醫療疏忽索償向私人客戶提供法律意見。

李旻軒律師精通英語、廣東話（粵語）及普通話。
