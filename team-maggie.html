Maggie Chan陳靜文律師

Founder & Principal Solicitor創辦人及首席律師

Biography簡歷

Maggie is the founder and principal solicitor of Chan Ching Man & Co.

Maggie obtained her Bachelor of Laws (LLB) at The University of Hong Kong and was admitted as a solicitor in Hong Kong in 2010. To expand her professional experience, she established her own firm in 2016 where she gives practical legal solutions to clients. Prior to founding Chan Ching Man & Co., Maggie was trained and worked in a reputed and time-honored local law firm in the general practice.

Maggie is committed to provide various kinds of dispute resolution services, whether via litigation, mediation and arbitration, Maggie sought to resolve the dispute is the most time and costs efficient way.

Through more than 15 years of practice, Maggie has developed extensive experience in representing and advising corporate and individual clients on various kinds of commercial and civil disputes including asset tracing and recovery, trademark, land, landlord and tenant, contract, shareholders, director's duties, tort, matrimonial and family, employment, personal injuries, estate administration, enforcement of award or judgment, interim measures or injunction application etc. Maggie also has experience in handling criminal defence and appeal in all levels of Courts.

In terms of non-contentious matters, Maggie provides advice and handles cases on conveyancing transactions, tenancy, probate and will, employment, company, compliance issues, data privacy, commercial agreements.

Maggie's clients come from across the globe other than local individuals, corporations (private or listed), institution and foreign high-net-worth individuals and corporations and advises on cross-border litigation or arbitration matters across Hong Kong, Mainland China and international markets.

Maggie is a civil celebrant of marriage and has celebrated a number of marriages. She is also accredited family mediator who has successfully resolved family disputes amicably by mediation.

Maggie's philosophy in her legal work is to provide clients with professional advice, practical and effective solutions, in order to assist clients to solve their problems, and most importantly, manage their risks before crisis arises.

Maggie reads and writes Chinese and English, speaks Cantonese, English and Mandarin.

陳靜文律師為陳靜文律師行的創辦人兼首席律師。

陳律師畢業於香港大學並獲頒法學士（LLB）學位，於 2010 年獲認許為香港執業律師。為進一步拓展專業領域，陳律師於 2016 年創立陳靜文律師行，致力為客戶提供切實可行的法律方案。創立本行前，陳律師曾於一家歷史悠久且享負盛名的本地全科律師行接受培訓及執業。

陳律師致力提供多元化的爭議解決服務；無論透過訴訟、調解或仲裁，均秉持以最具時間與成本效益的方式為客戶化解紛爭。

憑藉逾十五年的執業歷練，陳律師在代表企業及個人客戶處理各類商業與民事爭議方面積累了深厚經驗，範疇涵蓋：資產追蹤與追討、商標、土地、業主與租客、合約、股東爭議、董事責任、侵權、婚姻及家事法、勞資糾紛、人身傷害、遺產管理、仲裁裁決或判決之執行，以及申請臨時處分或禁制令等。此外，陳律師亦具備豐富經驗處理各級法院的刑事辯護與上訴案件。

在非訴訟法律事務方面，陳律師為各類案件提供專業法律意見及辦理相關手續，包括物業轉讓買賣、租務、遺囑與遺產承辦、僱傭事務、公司法務、合規諮詢、個人資料私隱及各類商業合約。

陳律師的客戶網絡遍及本地及全球，涵蓋個人、私人企業、上市公司、各類機構，以至海外高淨值人士及跨國企業；並經常就涉及香港、中國內地及國際市場的跨境訴訟與仲裁事宜提供策略性法律諮詢。

陳律師亦為香港婚姻監禮人，具備主持各類婚禮的豐富經驗；同時亦是認可家事調解員，曾多次透過調解協助客戶以和平協商的方式圓滿化解家庭爭議。

陳律師的執業理念在於提供精闢專業的法律意見與切實高效的解決方案，不僅協助客戶迎刃化解當前法律難題，更關鍵在於防患未然，在危機爆發前為客戶做好全面的風險管理。

陳律師精通中文及英文書寫；能操流利粵語、英語及普通話。
