Do Judges Take a Summer Holiday?法官都會放暑假？

The High Court is in recess every August — if you need to take legal steps over the summer, there are a few things to bear in mind.

高等法院每年8月休庭——如果你要在夏天進行法律程序，有幾點要留意。

Read article閱讀全文

1 September is not only the day students return to school after the summer holidays; it is also the day High Court judges return to the bench after their summer recess.

Under Order 64 of the Rules of the High Court, the Court of First Instance and the Court of Appeal are in recess each year between 1 August and 31 August. During the recess the two courts deal only with a limited range of matters, such as applications for injunctions and company winding-up, or causes, matters and applications which the Chief Justice of the Court of Final Appeal considers should be heard immediately or expeditiously.

Under Order 3 of the Rules of the High Court, unless the Court otherwise directs, the summer recess is excluded when computing the time for the service, filing or amendment of any pleading.

So if you intend to take any step in the High Court during the summer, do plan ahead.

每年的9月1日，除了是莘莘學子放完暑假後的開學日外，原來也是高等法院法官放假完暑假後的開庭日。

根據《高等法院規則》第64號命令，原訟法庭及上訴法庭在每年的8月1日及8月31日期間休庭，在休庭期間，原訟法庭及上訴法庭只會處理有限度事宜如禁制令申請、公司破產等，或終審法院首席法官決定有需要開庭即時或從速聆訊的訟案、事宜或申請。

根據《高等法院規則》第3號命令，除法庭另有指示外，在計算將任何狀書送達、送交存檔或修訂的期限時，夏天休庭期間不得計算在內。

如果你要在夏天期間於高等法院進行任何程序的話就要留意啦！

Criminal Litigation vs Civil Litigation刑事訴訟 VS 民事訴訟

Who brings criminal and civil proceedings, and what kinds of disputes do they cover? A short guide to the differences.

刑事與民事訴訟由誰提出、處理甚麼糾紛？一文看清兩者分別。

Read article閱讀全文

Litigation can broadly be divided into criminal proceedings and civil proceedings.

Criminal Proceedings

In Hong Kong, criminal proceedings are brought by the HKSAR Government before the courts to seek the conviction and sentencing of a person charged with an offence, so that they are held accountable for their unlawful conduct. Criminal prosecutions are the sole responsibility of the Secretary for Justice, who is not subject to directions from any other government department — a safeguard for judicial independence.

In deciding whether to prosecute, the Department of Justice mainly considers two questions:

  • Is there sufficient evidence to support the prosecution?
  • Is it in the public interest to bring the prosecution?

Straightforward prosecutions may be handled by the Police or other investigative agencies without the involvement of the Secretary for Justice, and may be reviewed in the Magistrates' Courts by Senior Court Prosecutors. Serious criminal offences such as murder, rape and armed robbery are generally tried in the Court of First Instance of the High Court by a judge sitting with a jury.

Civil Proceedings

Civil proceedings may be brought by the HKSAR Government or by any individual, institution, company or organisation seeking relief against the HKSAR Government or against any individual, institution, company or organisation — including claims for compensation and orders to resolve disputes concerning contracts, shareholders, employment, land and tenancies.

Civil cases mainly concern contract law, tort law, property law, administrative law, family law and revenue law:

  • Contract law governs the various agreements made between parties, including companies;
  • Tort law concerns claims arising from a breach of a duty of care owed to another person, for example by a shopkeeper;
  • Property law sets out and regulates rights of ownership over property and intellectual property, such as trade marks and copyright;
  • Administrative law protects individual rights against abuse of power by government bodies;
  • Family law covers divorce, custody of children and the division of assets;
  • Revenue law concerns the assessment and recovery of tax.

訴訟案件可以分為刑事訴訟和民事訴訟。

刑事訴訟

香港的刑事訴訟是會由香港特區政府向法庭提出，申請向被控犯罪人士定罪及判刑，令他們為作出的違法行為繩之於法。本港的刑事檢控工作是由律政司司長全權負責，並不會受制於其他政府部門的任何指令，確保司法獨立。

而律政司在決定是否對案件檢控時主要會考慮以下兩點：

  • 是否擁有充分證據支持起訴的法律程序？
  • 提出檢控是否符合公眾利益？

簡單的檢控工作可以由警方或其他調查機構處理而不需律政司司長的介入，並可在裁判法院經由高級法庭檢控主任審閱。而嚴重的刑事罪案，例如謀殺、強姦、持械行劫等，一般會由高等法院原訟法庭法官會同陪審團進行審訊。

民事訴訟

香港特區政府或任何個人、機構、公司或組織向法庭提出，申請向香港特區政府或任何個人、機構、公司或組織索償，及其他指示解決各方的紛爭，包括有關合同、股東、僱傭、土地、租賃等糾紛。

民事訴訟案件主要包括合約法、侵權法、財產法、行政法、家事法及稅務法：

  • 合約法關乎個人（包括公司）之間訂立的各種合約協議；
  • 侵權法關乎某人（如店家）在對另一人所負的謹慎責任上失職所引致的申索；
  • 財產法主要列明和規定對財產和知識產權（例如商標及版權）的擁有權等權利；
  • 行政法關乎個人權益的保障，防止政府機構濫用權力；
  • 家事法涉及離婚、子女管養權及財產分配等議題；
  • 稅務法是關乎評稅和追稅事宜。

Solicitors vs Barristers事務律師 VS 大律師

An overview of the differences between solicitors and barristers in Hong Kong's legal system.

介紹香港法律制度中事務律師與大律師的分別。

Read article查看圖解

In Hong Kong the legal profession is divided into solicitors and barristers, each with a distinct role.

Solicitors

Solicitors generally work in law firms, where they handle legal documents such as drafting contracts and preparing sale and purchase agreements. In Hong Kong, a solicitor's rights of audience before the courts are subject to certain restrictions.

Barristers

Barristers specialise in litigation and advocacy, representing clients and speaking in court. In Hong Kong, barristers enjoy rights of audience before all courts. Alongside their litigation work, barristers also provide clients with specialist legal advice.

Been in a Car Accident? What Should You Do?撞車點算好？

Five things a driver is legally required to do after a traffic accident in Hong Kong.

發生交通意外後，司機在法律上必須做的五件事。

Read article閱讀全文

Following a traffic accident, a driver must discharge the following duties:

1. Stop

Section 65(1) of the Road Traffic Ordinance provides that where an accident causes injury to another person, or damage to another vehicle or to property nearby, the driver must stop. The driver should remain at the scene for a sufficient period so that any person may obtain the relevant particulars from the driver.

2. Provide particulars

Under section 65(2) of the Road Traffic Ordinance, a driver involved in an accident must, when required, give a police officer the relevant particulars, including the driver's name and address, the name and address of the vehicle owner, and the vehicle's registration number. This is a personal duty: the driver must provide the particulars in person and cannot leave the scene after asking a passenger to provide them instead.

3. Report the accident

If there is no one at the scene to whom the driver can give particulars, the driver must, under section 56(2A) of the Road Traffic Ordinance, report the accident in person at the nearest police station within 24 hours.

4. Preserve evidence

Under section 57(1) of the Road Traffic Ordinance, in a serious accident causing death, serious injury or serious damage, it is an offence to destroy, alter or conceal any evidence.

5. Notify the insurer

Hong Kong law does not currently require a driver to report an accident to an insurer. However, most motor insurance policies require the insured to report an accident promptly if they intend to make a claim under the policy.

發生交通事故時，司機須履行以下責任：

1. 停車

《道路交通條例》第65(1)條訂明，凡因發生交通意外而對另一方造成傷害，或對附近另一車輛或物件造成損壞，司機必須停車。司機應在事故現場逗留一段足夠長的時間，以便任何人都可以從司機那裡獲取該駕駛者的相關詳情。

2. 提供詳情

根據《道路交通條例》第65(2)條，在發生意外時，司機有責任在被要求時向警務人員提供相關詳情，包括其姓名和地址、車主的姓名和地址，以及車輛的登記號碼。該職責屬於個人責任，因此司機必須親自提供相關詳情，而不能交託乘客提供有關詳情後自己離開現場。

3. 報告意外

如在事故現場沒有人可以讓司機提供詳情，司機則有責任根據《道路交通條例》第56(2A)條在事故發生後24小時內親自到最近的警署報案。

4. 保存證據

根據《道路交通條例》第57(1)條，在造成死亡或嚴重受傷或造成嚴重損壞的嚴重交通事故中，銷毀、更改或隱瞞任何證據均屬違法。

5. 通知保險公司

現時香港並沒有法律規定司機向保險公司報告事故。但是，大多數車輛保險政策都要求如果車主打算根據其保單索取賠償，發生意外後必須立即向保險公司報告事故。
