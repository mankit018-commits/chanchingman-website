Mr. Leon Chiu 趙英安律師

Associate Solicitor助理律師

Biography簡歷

Mr. Leon Chiu is a solicitor admitted in Hong Kong. He obtained his Graduate Diploma in Law from the University of the West of England and his Postgraduate Certificate in Laws (PCLL) from The University of Hong Kong. Prior to entering the legal profession, he gained valuable commercial and administrative experience with international professional firms and listed companies.

Mr. Chiu's practice focuses on civil and commercial litigation, dispute resolution and arbitration. He has extensive experience handling a broad range of contentious matters, including contractual disputes, property ownership disputes, family asset disputes, landlord and tenant disputes, adverse possession claims, matrimonial proceedings, personal injury actions and contentious probate matters. His experience also covers debt recovery actions, including loan/mortgage enforcement proceedings, various criminal proceedings and appeals.

In addition to his contentious practice, Mr. Chiu advises on non-contentious matters, including conveyancing, wills and probate, estate administration, wealth and succession planning, and tenancy agreements.

Throughout his practice, Mr. Chiu has acted for a diverse spectrum of clients, ranging from private individuals and families to high-net-worth individuals, landlords and property owners. His notable experience includes representing prominent landlords in commercial tenancy matters involving substantial properties in well-known developments in Hong Kong, as well as handling highly complex disputes concerning beneficial ownership of properties, family trusts, contentious probate, convertible share agreements involving listed companies, long-term tenancies and enforcement of judgment debts.

Mr. Chiu adopts a practical and commercially minded approach to legal practice, focusing on delivering effective and proportionate solutions tailored to his clients' needs. He is experienced in litigation strategy, settlement negotiations and mediation, with a view to resolving disputes efficiently while safeguarding his clients' interests.

Mr. Chiu is fluent in English, Cantonese and Putonghua.

趙英安律師為香港執業律師，於西英格蘭大學（University of the West of England, Bristol）法律深造文憑（Graduate Diploma in Law），並取得香港大學法學專業證書（PCLL）。在投身法律界之前，趙英安律師曾於國際知名專業機構及上市公司任職，累積了商業及行政管理方面的實務經驗。

趙英安律師的法律業務集中於民事及商業訴訟、爭議解決及仲裁。他在處理各類爭議性案件方面具備豐富經驗，包括合約糾紛、物業業權爭議、家族資產糾紛、業主與租客爭議、逆權侵佔、婚姻訴訟、人身傷害索償及具爭議性的遺產訴訟等。其執業範疇亦涵蓋債務追討，包括貸款及按揭執行訴訟，以及各類刑事訴訟及上訴案件。

除訴訟業務外，趙英安律師亦就各類非訴訟法律事務提供意見及服務，包括物業轉易、遺囑及遺產承辦、遺產管理、財富及傳承規劃，以及租賃協議等。

趙英安律師曾為不同背景及需求的客戶提供法律服務，客戶涵蓋一般個人及家庭、高資產淨值人士、業主及物業持有人。他曾代表知名業主處理涉及香港大型及著名物業發展項目的商業租賃事宜，亦曾處理多宗高度複雜的法律爭議，包括物業實益擁有權、家族信託、具爭議性的遺產訴訟、涉及上市公司的可換股股份協議糾紛、長期租賃爭議，以及判定債項的強制執行等。

趙英安律師處理法律事務時，著重務實及商業角度，致力按客戶的實際需要提供有效及相稱的法律解決方案。他在訴訟策略、和解談判及調解方面具備豐富經驗，致力在保障客戶權益的同時，以具成本效益的方式妥善解決爭議。

趙英安律師能操流利英語、粵語及普通話。
